OpenLondon — Sources & data

London mobility viewer, 100% client-side, no account, no key. All data comes from public APIs.

📷 TfL traffic cameras

  • Endpoint: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/Place/Type/JamCam (~882 cameras)
  • Media: s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/jamcams.tfl.gov.uk/{id}.jpg|.mp4
  • Data © Transport for London (Open Data)

⚠️ Incidents / disruptions

  • Endpoint: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/Road/All/Disruption (~106)
  • Coloured by severity: Minor / Moderate / Severe / Closure

🚇 Transport

  • Endpoints: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/StopPoint/Mode/{tube|national-rail|dlr|overground|elizabeth-line|tram|bus}
