OpenLondon — Sources & data

Last updated: August 2026.

London mobility viewer, 100% client-side, no account, no key. All data comes from public APIs with per-provider attribution.

Traffic cameras

  • Endpoint: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/Place/Type/JamCam (~882 cameras)
  • Media: s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/jamcams.tfl.gov.uk/{id}.jpg|.mp4
  • Data © Transport for London (Open Data)

Incidents / disruptions

  • Endpoint: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/Road/All/Disruption (~106)
  • Coloured by severity: Minor / Moderate / Severe / Closure

Transport

  • Endpoints: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/StopPoint/Mode/{tube|national-rail|dlr|overground|elizabeth-line|tram|bus}
  • One call per network, cached for 24 h (respects TfL rate limits).

Map data

  • OpenStreetMap © OSM contributors (ODbL)
  • Google Satellite / Hybrid © Google
  • Esri World Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics
  • CARTO basemaps © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO
  • Great-Britain relief (DTM) — Environment Agency, WMS lidar-composite-digital-terrain-model-dtm-1m, OGL v3.0. Source: Environment Agency — updated 2026.

Geocoding

  • TfL search: GET api.tfl.gov.uk/StopPoint/Search?query=
  • OSM/Nominatim (London-bounded) — © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
