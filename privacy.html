OpenLondon — Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: August 2026.

Data collection

OpenLondon is a static application that runs entirely in your browser. No personal data is collected, transmitted or sold.

Local storage

Some preferences (saved places, active networks, cached data) are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device.

Network calls

The app calls public third-party APIs: Transport for London (mobility data), OSM/Nominatim (geocoding), and tile servers (OSM, Google, Esri, CARTO, Environment Agency). These services apply their own policies.

Cookies

OpenLondon does not install any tracking cookies. Tile and API providers may set their own cookies according to their policy.

No tracking

No tracker, no analytics, no pixels. The site shows no advertising.
