OpenLondon — Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: August 2026.

Data collection

OpenLondon is a static application that runs entirely in your browser. No personal data is collected, transmitted or sold. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Local storage

Some preferences (saved places, active networks, cached data) are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Network calls

The app calls public third-party APIs and tile servers: Transport for London (mobility data), OSM/Nominatim (geocoding), and tile providers (OSM, Google, Esri, CARTO, Environment Agency). These services apply their own policies and may log requests to their servers.

Cookies

OpenLondon does not install any tracking cookies. Tile and API providers may set their own cookies according to their respective policies.

No tracking

No tracker, no analytics, no pixels. The site shows no advertising.
